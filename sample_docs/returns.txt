Northwind Outfitters — Returns & Refunds Policy

We want you to shop with confidence. If you're not happy with your purchase,
you can return most new, unused items within 30 days of delivery for a full
refund to your original payment method.

To start a return, log in to your account and open the order you want to
return, then select "Request return". You'll get a prepaid shipping label by
email within one business day. Pack the item in its original packaging and
drop it at any carrier location.

Refunds are issued within 5 business days after we receive and inspect the
returned item. You'll get an email confirmation once the refund is processed.

Some items can't be returned: final-sale items marked at checkout, gift cards,
and opened personal-care products. If an item arrives damaged or defective,
contact support within 14 days and we'll cover return shipping and send a
replacement or refund, whichever you prefer.

Holiday extension: purchases made between November 1 and December 25 can be
returned until January 31 of the following year.
