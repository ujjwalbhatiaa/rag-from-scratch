Northwind Outfitters — Shipping Information

Orders placed before 2 PM Eastern ship the same business day from our Toronto
warehouse. Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days and costs $6.95, and
it's free on orders over $75.

Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs $14.95. We currently
ship to all Canadian provinces and territories. Shipping to the Yukon,
Northwest Territories, and Nunavut adds 2 to 4 extra business days.

You'll receive a tracking number by email as soon as your order ships. If
your tracking hasn't updated in 3 business days, contact support and we'll
investigate with the carrier.

During the holiday season (December 1 to December 24), order by December 18
for guaranteed standard delivery before December 25 in Ontario, Quebec, and
the western provinces.
