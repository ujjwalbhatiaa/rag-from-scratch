Northwind Outfitters — Membership & Pricing

Northwind Plus is our membership program at $9.99 per month or $99 per year.
Members get free express shipping on every order, early access to seasonal
sales, and 5% back in store credit on all purchases.

Students with a valid university email get Northwind Plus at half price:
$4.99 per month. The discount applies for up to four years and renews
automatically each year you re-verify.

You can cancel anytime from your account settings. If you cancel within the
first 14 days, you get a full refund of the membership fee. After that,
refunds are prorated to the unused full months.

Store credit from the 5% member reward never expires and can be combined with
sale prices and coupon codes at checkout.
